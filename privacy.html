What we collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, email, phone number, business details and project information, when you fill in a form, email or call us.

We also collect basic analytics data such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotes
  • To deliver and manage your project
  • To send invoices and important updates
  • To improve our website and services

Marketing

We’ll only send you marketing emails if you opt in, and every email includes an unsubscribe link, in line with the Spam Act 2003.

Sharing your information

We never sell your information. We only share it with trusted providers (like hosting, email and payment services) who help us run our business.

Storage & security

Your information is stored securely and only accessed by team members who need it.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics and to improve your experience. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings.

Complaints
